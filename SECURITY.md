# Security Policy

## Reporting a Vulnerability

If you find a security issue in one of our open-source projects, please
report it privately rather than opening a public issue.

Email **hello@applift.xyz** with:

- A description of the issue and its potential impact
- Steps to reproduce, or a proof of concept if you have one
- The project and version affected

We'll acknowledge your report within a few business days and keep you
updated as we investigate and fix it. We ask that you give us a reasonable
amount of time to address the issue before disclosing it publicly.

Thank you for helping keep our projects and their users safe.
